What to expect

  • Nothing runs until you confirm it. Reading your portfolio is automatic. Anything that changes something — a cancel, an order, a new rule — stops and shows you exactly what it's about to do, built from the real values it will send, not from its own description of them.
  • It can only do what you can. Actions go through the same code and the same checks as the buttons elsewhere in Cyrus, as you. It can't reach another account, and it can't place a market order or withdraw to an address you haven't whitelisted, because Cyrus itself can't.
  • Automation rules are the ones to read twice. A rule fires later, on its own, possibly while you're asleep — and a withdraw rule moves funds off the exchange. Check the trigger and the threshold on the confirmation card before approving.
  • Not financial advice. It carries out what you ask and describes what you hold; it won't tell you what to buy or sell. Like any language model it can be wrong — check anything that matters.
  • Pennies per question. A portfolio snapshot is small; typical questions cost a fraction of a cent against your Anthropic credit.
  • Conversations stay on this device. History is kept in the app's local storage, per account, never sent anywhere and never written to your Cyrus database. Clear it any time from the history panel.
