Transfer history is fetched from each exchange and stored locally so this page loads instantly. Movements between two connected exchanges are paired automatically — by shared transaction hash where one exists, otherwise by matching the amount and timing, allowing for network fees. Anything else needs an address you have labelled under Wallets: an exchange reports the address a transfer went to, but never who owns it, so an unlabelled counterparty is shown as unknown rather than guessed at.
